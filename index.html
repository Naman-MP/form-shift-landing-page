Useful answers

Before you ask.

How long does an order take?+

Because each object is made to order, production usually takes a few working days before dispatch. Exact timing is confirmed with your order.

Will every print look identical?+

Small layer lines and minor variations are part of the 3D-printing process. Every piece is checked for fit and function before packing.

Can I request another colour?+

Yes. Send a colour reference with your order request. Availability and any price difference will be confirmed before printing.

Are these toys for small children?+

No. These are desk objects and may contain small or moving parts. Keep them away from children under three and heat sources.
